Methods

Energy is required for sample-clock alignment. Applying settings batches changes into one rescue decoder restart; its statistics reset. Readsb and FR24 stay running. Optional methods send only validated, nonduplicate packets to FR24 when enabled. Broad CRC candidates remain quarantined.

DSP extras by distance

Energy has its own method column. DSP extras is the deduplicated, feed-eligible total across all enabled methods, including Stream1090; method columns are its exclusive attribution breakdown. Stream1090 candidates pass the same clock, CRC, prior-stock ICAO, conflict and cross-method duplicate gates before counting or relay. DSP uplift = 100 × DSP extras ÷ stock DF17 in the same aligned RF windows. Broad CRC research stays separate, is excluded from both totals and uplift, and never enters the feeder stream. Disabled methods with no recorded extras have no columns; historical counts remain visible. Distance uses a prior stock position no older than 15 seconds; unknown positions stay separate.
