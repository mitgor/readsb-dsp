Interpretation and next data needed

The candidate improves a few isolated packet reconstructions, but the enabled DSP/Stream1090 union already covers them on this RF. The far-range training and validation sample is too small. Prefer stock-supported difficult distant windows, independent waveform/timestamp checks, and time-spread RF with complete stock/accepted-output journals. Keep all uncertain weak/overlap cases quarantined. No collection change was made here.

  • Only eight new qualifying >=180nm train windows, one validation window, zero new test windows; no far-range generalization claim. Historical far-range labels were not used for new far weighting.
  • Field RF was reserved before fitting but recorded before checkpoint freeze; not post-freeze prospective data. Field RF is chronological, not ICAO-disjoint; 23 stock packets repeat a training payload. Window splits are ICAO/payload/IQ-disjoint.
  • Stock-assisted CRC-valid labels are not independent physical ground truth. All CRC research candidates remain quarantined; none had same-transmission stock confirmation.
  • Some archives contain open/unclosed packet journals; only individually closed, stream-complete continuous RF bursts enter this experiment.
  • Baseline is full enabled serial replay plus actual stock/unified masks, not a claim that historical archive routing matched current routing. Replay includes all three old neural models, coherent pair retries and Stream1090.
  • Serial algorithm CPU includes duplicate frontend work across replay branches and Stream1090 children; excludes live transport/network overhead and is not whole-device electrical power.
  • No hyperparameter or model tuning followed the chronological RF replay. Additional controls only audited the already frozen candidates.
