## Interpret the SIC-on control carefully

The low-priority SIC-on shadow control decoded the full 320-reference set but emitted 263 and 172 before its 3-second admission deadlines. Those 57/148 copy deadline misses reflect this duplicate-work, resource-constrained replay. They do not establish packet loss in the unmodified, higher-priority production pipeline. SIC-off synchronous and asynchronous copies each emitted all 319 eligible extras in both runs. The input clock was 1× in every completed condition; no 12× dilation was used.

## Correctness fix discovered at native speed

The former completion tap could miss a future that became ready and was removed by normal grouping during the same pop call. The native test refused a grouping-parity assertion. The replacement snapshots pending future references, collects before and after normal grouping, and preserves raw seam events without pre-deduplication. It still publishes ready producers without waiting for slow unrelated futures. Two targeted regression tests cover this race and independent ready/slow futures. Original decoder grouping matches exactly in all six completed comparisons. No C/Rust decoder, neural model or admission threshold changed.

## Conditions and preservation

Three modes were tested twice on identical 16 captured RF windows with one known RF-copy gap excluded: sync+SIC, sync−SIC and async−SIC. Test topology mirrors production: two DSP workers on 2/3, coherent workers on 0/1. The isolated process uses nice 19, CPUWeight 10, a 200% Linux CPU ceiling across four cores and 1 GiB memory. Models/libraries/source/config identities were checked against the current receiver. Original readsb/FR24/DSP/relay/ADSBx/collector PIDs, gain 38.6 dB, sample-loss counters, configuration and 5 GB RF cap are protected.

The first restrictive test and a test-tail/grouping race failure are preserved and excluded from the completed comparisons. During the repeated sequence FR24 briefly disconnected, causing the owned benchmark guard to stop its child. FR24 reconnected without a restart; the remaining conditions were rerun after reconnect. Evidence logs and numeric feeder events preserve this interruption; it is not silently counted as uninterrupted coverage. Only test copies had SIC disabled; production remains unchanged.

CPU sums measured worker CPU, Stream1090 child CPU and coordinator CPU during native feed/drain; initialization and post-test reference audits are excluded. Stock context here is the captured valid DF17 stream, not every possible ModeS/control byte. Latency is a local replay RF-clock estimate with captured readiness alignment and scheduler quantization, not RF-to-cloud delivery. Repeated engineering RF is evaluation-only, never training labels.

The result supports the lighter SIC-off asynchronous profile for a longer isolated trial. It does not authorize or perform production activation, prove FR24 backend acceptance, establish a distant range gain or measure electrical power.
